Help / How to use CampCare
A quick, step-by-step guide to every part of CampCare — for health workers and supervisors.
For health workers
1
Registering a Family & Child

Screenshot: Register family & child page
Frontend/images/help/register-family.png

Add a guardian and one or more children during a home visit. If the family already has a child on file, use "Existing family" instead so you don't have to re-type the guardian's details.

    1Click 'Register family & child' in the sidebar.
    2For a new family, enter the guardian's name, phone number, and area. If the family is already registered, switch to 'Existing family' and select them from the list.
    3Enter the child's name and date of birth. Click '+ Add another child' to register more than one child at once.
    4Click 'Save & generate schedule' and the vaccination schedule is created automatically.

2
Checking Who's Due for a Vaccine

Screenshot: Due / overdue list page
Frontend/images/help/due-overdue-list.png

See every child in your zone with a color-coded status — at a glance you'll know who needs a visit this week.

    1Click 'Due / overdue list' in the sidebar.
    2Green means the child is up to date.
    3Gold means a vaccine is due soon.
    4Red means overdue — visit this family as soon as possible.
    5Click on a child's name to open their full vaccination record.

3
Scheduling a Camp

Screenshot: Schedule camp page
Frontend/images/help/schedule-camp.png

Plan an outreach vaccination camp for your zone so families know where and when to come.

    1Click 'Schedule camp' in the sidebar.
    2Enter the location and date (e.g. a union parishad ground or school field).
    3Click 'Add camp' and it will appear in 'Camp list'.
    4On the day of the camp, go to 'Camp attendance' to check families in.

4
Sending Reminders

Screenshot: Send reminder page
Frontend/images/help/send-reminder.png

Broadcast a Telegram reminder to every registered family in a zone ahead of a camp date.

    1Click 'Send reminder' in the sidebar.
    2From the dropdown, select the camp you want to send a reminder for.
    3Click 'Send Telegram reminder' to message every registered family at once.

For supervisors
5
Supervisor Dashboard

Screenshot: Coverage-gap dashboard page
Frontend/images/help/supervisor-dashboard.png

Get a bird's-eye view of vaccination coverage across every health worker's zone, and spot which areas need more outreach.

    1Log in as a supervisor and the 'Coverage-gap dashboard' opens automatically.
    2Coverage bars are shown per zone — red or gold bars mean that zone needs more attention.
    3Open 'Multi-worker overview' to see each health worker's registration and vaccination counts.
    4Open 'Performance report' for a closer look at each worker's families, doses given, coverage rate, and camps run.
